# Voice-of-customer quote bank

These are exact quotes from customers, grouped by theme. Use them for positioning, messaging, and interview prompts.

**Where the quotes come from and how far to trust them:**

- **Exact text:** quotes from the Chrome Web Store, the App Store, AppSumo, Hacker News, Bluesky, and Lemmy were taken from raw data and are word for word.
- **Check before reuse:** quotes from Trustpilot and the OpenAI forum went through a tool that may have summarized the page. Check them against the live page before you publish them.
- **Spelling:** typos are kept exactly as the customers wrote them.
- **Full context:** each quote is listed with its source in the `sources/` files.
